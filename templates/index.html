Why Session ID is 100% Ban-Proof:

By using your browser's existing Instagram login cookie (sessionid), Instagram sees the request coming from your authorized session without triggering security checkpoints.

How to get your sessionid in 30 seconds
  1. Open instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Press F12 → Go to Application (or Storage) → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com.
  3. Find the cookie named sessionid and copy its Value.
